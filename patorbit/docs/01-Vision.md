# Patorbit Vision

## Mission

Patorbit is a knowledge-centric platform that helps people and organizations build trust through structured knowledge instead of isolated documents.

## Problem

Today's systems store information but rarely capture the reasoning behind it. As a result:

- Knowledge is fragmented.
- Trust is difficult to establish.
- Claims are hard to verify.
- Evidence is disconnected.
- Decisions lack transparency.

## Solution

Patorbit organizes knowledge around six core concepts:

1. Identity
2. Claim
3. Evidence
4. Reasoning
5. Confidence
6. Trust

These concepts form a connected knowledge graph that enables explainable, trustworthy decision-making.

## Primary Users

- Individuals
- Recruiters
- Organizations
- Researchers
- Developers

## Long-Term Goal

Create the world's most trusted knowledge platform where every claim can be explained, every decision can be justified, and trust is measurable.
