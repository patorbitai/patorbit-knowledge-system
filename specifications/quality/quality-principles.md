# Quality Principles

## Purpose

Core quality engineering principles for the Patorbit platform.

## Principles

### 1. Quality is Everyone's Responsibility

Quality is not a phase or a role — it is embedded in every role and every stage of development.

### 2. Shift Left

Find defects as early as possible — at design time, during development, and at commit time.

### 3. Automation First

Tests are automated wherever practical. Manual testing is reserved for exploration and usability.

### 4. Risk-Based Prioritization

Test effort is prioritized based on business risk, user impact, and failure probability.

### 5. Measurable Quality

Quality is defined by measurable metrics, not subjective opinion. Every team tracks and improves quality KPIs.

### 6. Continuous Quality

Quality is not a gate — it is a continuous process of improvement throughout the development lifecycle.

### 7. Traceability

Every test traces back to a requirement, user story, or risk. Every defect traces back to a root cause.

### 8. Reliability by Design

Quality includes reliability — systems are tested for failure scenarios, not just happy paths.
