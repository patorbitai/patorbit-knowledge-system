# Claim Analysis

## Purpose

AI-powered analysis of professional claims to assess consistency, relevance, and completeness.

## Capabilities

- **Consistency Check**: Flag inconsistencies between claims (e.g., overlapping dates, conflicting titles).
- **Completeness Assessment**: Identify missing information (description, skills, dates).
- **Relevance Scoring**: Assess how relevant a claim is for a target role.
- **Optimization Suggestions**: Suggest improvements to claim phrasing.

## Analysis Sources

- User's existing claims.
- Evidence metadata.
- Industry standards and taxonomies.
- Target role requirements.
