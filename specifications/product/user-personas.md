# User Personas

## Purpose

Define target customer segments and detailed personas.

## Personas

### Individual Professional

**Goal**: Create a trusted, comprehensive Career Passport.
**Needs**: Claim management, evidence upload, verification, AI resume builder.
**Pains**: Time-consuming resume updates, unverifiable claims, manual applications.

### Recruiter

**Goal**: Find and evaluate qualified candidates efficiently.
**Needs**: Advanced search, verified claims, candidate matching, outreach.
**Pains**: Unverifiable resumes, time spent screening, poor candidate data.

### Organization Admin

**Goal**: Verify employee claims and manage talent data.
**Needs**: Claim verification, credential issuance, workforce analytics.
**Pains**: Manual verification processes, no central talent data.

### Enterprise Customer

**Goal**: Integrate verified career data into existing HR systems.
**Needs**: API access, custom integrations, SSO, compliance.
**Pains**: Fragmented talent data, lack of verification.

### Verifier

**Goal**: Verify evidence for claims accurately.
**Needs**: Evidence review tools, decision workflows, audit trails.
**Pains**: Lack of structured verification processes.
