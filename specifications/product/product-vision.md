# Product Vision

## Purpose

Define the long-term product vision for the Patorbit platform.

## Vision Statement

To become the world's most trusted Career Intelligence Platform, where every professional achievement is verifiable, every career decision is informed, and every opportunity is accessible.

## Strategic Pillars

| Pillar           | Description                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trust**        | Every claim is verifiable. Every verification is auditable. Every identity is authentic. |
| **Intelligence** | AI-powered insights guide career decisions, resume optimization, and job matching.       |
| **Ecosystem**    | A two-sided marketplace connecting talent with opportunities through trusted data.       |
| **Lifelong**     | From first job to retirement, the platform supports every career stage.                  |

## Vision Evolution

| Phase                     | Timeframe | Focus                                                             |
| ------------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Phase 1: Foundation**   | Year 1    | Passport creation, verification, resume builder                   |
| **Phase 2: Intelligence** | Year 2    | AI coaching, career recommendations, recruiter tools              |
| **Phase 3: Ecosystem**    | Year 3    | Talent marketplace, enterprise APIs, credential network           |
| **Phase 4: Autonomous**   | Year 4+   | Autonomous career agents, predictive modeling, industry standards |
