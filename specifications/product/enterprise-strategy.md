# Enterprise Strategy

## Purpose

Enterprise go-to-market and product strategy.

## Enterprise Value Proposition

- Verified talent data reduces hiring cost and risk.
- API-first platform integrates with existing HR systems.
- Trusted credentials for compliance and background screening.

## Enterprise Features

- **SSO/SAML**: Enterprise identity integration.
- **API Platform**: Custom integrations with ATS, HRIS, CRM.
- **Custom Verification**: Configure verification workflows.
- **Analytics**: Workforce skills intelligence.
- **SLA**: Guaranteed uptime and support.

## Sales Model

- Self-serve for teams.
- Sales-assisted for enterprise.
- Partner-led for geographic expansion.
