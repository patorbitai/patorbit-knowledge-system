# Governance

## Purpose

Product governance, decision-making, and review processes.

## Product Council

| Role               | Responsibility                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| CPO / Product Lead | Product strategy, roadmap prioritization |
| Engineering Lead   | Technical feasibility, estimation        |
| Design Lead        | User experience, design system           |
| Data Lead          | Metrics, experimentation                 |
| Business Lead      | Revenue, partnerships                    |

## Decision Framework

| Decision         | Approver                       | Criteria                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Feature priority | Product Council                | Business value, effort, risk    |
| Pricing change   | CPO + Business                 | Market research, unit economics |
| Platform change  | Product Council + Architecture | Technical impact                |
| Partnership      | CPO + Business                 | Strategic alignment             |

## Review Cadence

| Review              | Frequency   | Participants                 |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------- |
| Roadmap Review      | Monthly     | Product Council              |
| Feature Review      | Bi-weekly   | Product, Design, Engineering |
| Post-Release Review | Per release | Product, Engineering         |
| Strategy Review     | Quarterly   | All                          |
