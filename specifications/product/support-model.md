# Support Model

## Purpose

Customer support model for the platform.

## Support Tiers

| Tier         | Availability   | Channels              | SLA      |
| ------------ | -------------- | --------------------- | -------- |
| Free         | Business hours | Email, Knowledge Base | 48 hours |
| Professional | Business hours | Email, Chat           | 24 hours |
| Team         | 12/5           | Email, Chat, Phone    | 8 hours  |
| Enterprise   | 24/7           | Dedicated support     | 1 hour   |

## Knowledge Base

- Self-service help center.
- Structured by product area.
- Searchable with AI-powered answers.

## Feedback Loop

- Support tickets → product improvements.
- User feedback → feature requests.
- Bug reports → engineering.
