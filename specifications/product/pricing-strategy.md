# Pricing Strategy

## Purpose

Pricing strategy for the Patorbit platform.

## Model

**Value-based pricing**: Align price with the value delivered to each persona.

## Pricing Tiers

| Tier         | Target Persona | Value Metric                  |
| ------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Free         | Individual     | Core passport                 |
| Professional | Individual     | AI features, career growth    |
| Team         | Organization   | Verification, recruiter tools |
| Enterprise   | Enterprise     | API, integration, custom      |

## Pricing Principles

- **Simple**: Easy to understand.
- **Scalable**: Grows with the customer.
- **Fair**: Based on value, not features.
- **Predictable**: No surprise charges.
