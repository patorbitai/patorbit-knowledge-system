# Operational Runbooks

## Purpose

Standard runbook templates for operational procedures.

## Runbook Template

```markdown
# [Runbook Title]

**Owner**: [Team]
**Last Updated**: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Overview

[What this runbook is for.]

## Prerequisites

[Tools, permissions, etc.]

## Steps

1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

## Validation

[How to confirm success.]

## Rollback

[How to undo the changes.]

## Escalation

[Who to contact for help.]
```
