## Policy Format

Each policy includes:

- **Purpose**: Why the policy exists.
- **Scope**: Who and what it covers.
- **Policy Statements**: Specific requirements.
- **Enforcement**: How compliance is measured.
- **Exceptions**: Process for requesting exceptions.
